Debt collection was not a built family in `data/rebuilds.json`. Credit control inside a business (Chaser) already existed; this build is the third-party collection agency: clients who list accounts, collectors who work them within the contact rules, arrangements, trust money and remittances.

Scores are judgement, not measured demand. [Collect!'s cloud pricing page](https://www.collect.org/solutions/pricing-cloud/) (checked 19 October 2026) publishes Australian dollar prices: Entrepreneur from A$9,690 a year with 3 users, Professional from A$33,390 a year with 10 users, Corporate from A$69,390 a year with 20 users, and A$1,390 a year for each extra user. The same page shows US$6,990, US$24,000 and US$49,950. A 10-collector agency on Professional starts at A$33,390 a year before letter services, texting, payment processing and dialler accounts, which are separate.

The buyer fit: collection agencies in both countries, and the recovery desks inside law and accounting firms, already in our professional-firms industry. The rules are local and written down (RG 96 in Australia, the Limitation Act 2010 in New Zealand), which makes the compliance checks real rather than generic.
